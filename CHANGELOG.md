# Changelog

## 5.1.0 (2026-11-25)

26 new tools, bringing the total to 429, and 43 more calculators (306 in all): simulation labs for mechanisms, mechanics, waves and heat, optics, geometry, genetics and biology; a PDF reader that annotates, a PDF toolbox and PDF comparison; flipbooks whose pages turn like paper; worksheets and solvers that show their working; a code runner; Paint; more boards, parts and analyses in the Circuit Simulator; and many more formats in the File Converter.

- **Start screen:** Benchtop opens on the list of all tools again; Settings → Start screen makes it open on Notes instead. The setting is kept under a new name, so the Notes start that 5.0 saved for everyone does not carry over.
- **Simulation labs (new category):** Mechanism Simulator (planar linkages drawn from pivots, bars, cranks and sliders, or classics from the four-bar to Jansen's Strandbeest leg, with coupler curves, position, velocity and acceleration plots, Grashof type, transmission angle and where a linkage locks; saved as designs, CSV and SVG); Mechanics Lab (projectiles with air drag on four worlds, the pendulum at any swing, a driven spring–mass–damper with its resonance curve, air-track collisions, Kepler orbits, a block on a slope, levers and pulleys, adding forces); Waves, Heat and Fields Lab (beats, standing waves on strings and in pipes, the Doppler effect, a gas of particles against Maxwell–Boltzmann and Boyle's law, field lines and equipotentials, generators, buoyancy and Bernoulli); Optics Bench (ray diagrams for lenses and mirrors, Fresnel's equations, critical and Brewster angles, prisms, slits and gratings, Malus's law); Geometry Lab (triangle centres with Euler's line and the nine-point circle, polygons, circle theorems, transformations with their matrices, conics, the unit circle); Genetics Lab (Punnett squares for up to three genes with incomplete dominance, codominance, ABO and X-linked inheritance; selection, mutation and drift with a Hardy–Weinberg test; DNA transcribed and translated, with mutations named; chi-square tests); Biology Lab (population growth and harvesting, predator and prey cycles, SIR and SEIR epidemics with vaccination, enzyme kinetics with inhibitors and Lineweaver–Burk fits to your data, a Hodgkin–Huxley neuron, diffusion and osmosis).
- **Mechanical engineering:** Cams, Geneva Drive and Gear Trains (new: cam profiles from five motion programs for knife-edge, roller and flat-faced followers with pressure angle and undercut checks, saved as DXF, SVG or CSV; an animated Geneva drive; compound and planetary gear trains); Engine and Power Cycles (new: Otto, Diesel, dual, Brayton with real compressor and turbine and a regenerator, Carnot, Stirling and Rankine, every state and process on P–v and T–s diagrams that animate); Pneumatic and Hydraulic Circuits (new: ISO 1219 symbols, valves with every kind of operation, single- and double-acting cylinders, flow controls and logic valves, run with lines filling and cylinders moving).
- **Circuit Simulator:** the Arduino Nano and Mega 2560, the ATtiny85 and the Raspberry Pi Pico join the Uno and the ESP32. The Pico runs Arduino sketches and, from a MicroPython .uf2 you download, a Python program edited in its panel; its boot ROM is built from Raspberry Pi's source. Sensors (DHT22, BME280, MPU6050, HC-SR04, PIR, light sensor, thermistor, 4×4 keypad, rotary encoder, joystick, DS1307/DS3231 clock), displays (16×2 LCDs, parallel or I²C, SSD1306 OLED, ILI9341 TFT, MAX7219 LED matrix, NeoPixel rings and strips) and servos, steppers and 74HC595 shift registers answer the same pin, I²C and SPI signals as the real parts. Analyses as in CircuitLab: DC operating point, DC sweep, transient, and AC with Bode plots, with parameter steps, spectra, cursors and CSV, and parts for them: the four controlled sources, a transformer, JFETs, a comparator, a three-terminal regulator and a Laplace transfer-function block. 61 example circuits in 11 groups. In the desktop app, sketches for the new boards compile and upload with arduino-cli (the Arduino AVR boards, ATTinyCore and Earle Philhower's Pico core).
- **Electronics:** PLC Ladder Logic Simulator (new: rungs written as conditions and actions run as a ladder diagram whose wires light up, with set and reset coils, timers, counters and edge contacts, six examples and structured text out) and Karnaugh Map Solver (new: 2 to 6 variables, minterms with don't cares or an expression, the smallest sum of products and product of sums with the groups on the map, prime implicants, truth table, C or Verilog).
- **PDF Reader (new):** thumbnails, contents and search with every match highlighted; highlight, underline and strike out text; notes, text boxes, pen strokes, rectangles, ellipses, lines and arrows, with colours, comments, undo and keyboard shortcuts. Saved as real PDF annotations that other readers show and edit (also on turned pages), or flattened into the pages; comments can be listed as Markdown.
- **PDF Toolbox (new):** the tools of Stirling PDF that Benchtop lacked: bookmarks (contents) editing, attached files, laying another PDF over or under the pages, colours (contrast, greyscale, invert, replace), inserting and removing blank pages (pages with any text are kept unless you say otherwise), removing comments, links, pictures and signatures, sanitising scripts and metadata, flattening, unlocking and editing form fields, repair, everything in a file with its JavaScript, an accessibility check, pictures and scanned photos taken out, one long page, paper size and scale, turning sideways pages upright, pictures and QR codes stamped on pages, naming a file from its title, and an RFC 3161 timestamp.
- **PDF Compare (new):** two versions of a PDF compared word by word and as pictures: the words removed and added, marked on the pages and listed, a picture of what changed on every page with a choice of detail and strictness, a Markdown report, and either version saved with its changes highlighted.
- **PDF Passwords and Compression:** repairs damaged PDFs, turns them grey with text kept as text, and takes every picture out.
- **Flipbook Editor (new):** page-turn publications like FlipHTML5 and Heyzine, made offline from PDFs, pictures and blank pages: reorder, insert and delete pages, hard covers, single pages or spreads, right-to-left books, hotspots that open links, go to pages, play video or sound from your own files or show notes, a contents list, background, page-turn sound, and a kiosk mode that turns pages by itself and goes back to the cover when nobody touches it. Projects are saved as .flipbook files, and a flipbook exports as one HTML file that works offline with nothing to install. Pages turn with StPageFlip.
- **Flipbook Reader (new):** PDFs and .flipbook files read as books, with contents, page pictures, search in a PDF's text, zoom, full screen and the keyboard.
- **Notes:** sketches and mind maps inside notes: boxes, bubbles, clouds, sticky notes, arrows that stay stuck to shapes, and a pen, drawn by hand (Rough.js) or clean. The format is described in docs/NOTE-FORMAT.md.
- **Maths and science:** Calcpad Worksheet (new: engineering calculations written as plain text and read as a typeset report, every formula with its values put in and its result, with units, input boxes, conditions, loops, solvers, plots and maps; saved as HTML or PDF); Calculus Solver (new: derivatives, integrals including improper ones, limits, Taylor series, extrema, tangents, differential equations and Riemann sums, each with worked steps, a graph and a check of the answer); Chemistry Solver (new: equations balanced, also redox in acid or base and by half-reactions, stoichiometry with the limiting reagent, empirical formulas, oxidation states, pH, ICE tables, kinetics, titration curves, galvanic cells, gases and solutions, with worked steps); Kundli and Panchang (new: a Vedic birth chart in North or South Indian style with Lahiri positions, nakshatras and padas, divisional charts, Vimshottari dashas and Ashtakoota matching, and the panchang of any day and place, in English or Hindi).
- **Code Runner (new):** write a program, give it input and run it, like an online compiler but offline. Python (Pyodide), JavaScript, TypeScript, Lua and SQL (SQLite or DuckDB) run in the app; the desktop app also runs C, C++, Java, C#, Go, Rust, Kotlin, PHP, Ruby, Bash, R, Perl, Swift and Dart with the compilers installed on your computer, each run in a fresh temporary folder with a time limit.
- **Pictures, sound and code drawing:** Paint (new: the classic Windows Paint tools, from pencil, airbrush and fill to text, shapes, selections and crop, with resize, rotate, flip, invert, a zoomed pixel grid and PNG, JPEG, WebP or BMP); Ringtone Composer (new: monophonic ringtones keyed in like on the phones of the late 1990s, or typed as RTTTL or composer notation, played as square-wave beeps, transposed, saved as WAV, MIDI or RTTTL); D3.js Editor (new: D3 code beside its chart, with your CSV, TSV and JSON files read by d3.csv and the rest, run in a sealed frame; saves SVG, PNG or a web page with the data inside); anime.js Editor (new: anime.js code beside its animation; saves a web page that runs on its own, or SVG and PNG).
- **File Converter:** HEIC and HEIF photos converted inside the app (libheif), without location or camera details; e-mails (.eml) and saved web pages (.mht) to web pages or PDFs with their pictures, remote content blocked; pictures traced into SVG; PostScript and EPS to PDF or PNG (Ghostscript); XPS and OpenXPS to PDF (MuPDF); Visio, Publisher, CorelDRAW and other drawings to PDF, PNG, SVG or ODG; macro-enabled Word, Excel and PowerPoint files, templates and slide shows, Pages, Numbers and Keynote, WPS, Hangul and Lotus Word Pro documents (LibreOffice); PDF to Word, ODT or RTF; and RAR, 7z, ISO, CAB and other archives repacked as ZIP, 7z or .tar.gz with bsdtar (built into macOS and Windows), with TAR archives to ZIP built in. The command line has the new `postscript`, `xps` and `archive` recipes too.
- **Pictures and archives:** the picture tools open HEIC and HEIF photos from phones, and ZIP and Archives opens .bz2 files.
- **Calculator library:** 43 more calculators, 306 in all: machine design (bearing life, column buckling, Mohr's circle, springs, bolted, riveted and welded joints, power screws, governors, flywheels, clutches and brakes, thin and thick pressure vessels, fatigue by Goodman, Soderberg and Gerber, Marin factors, Miner's rule, fracture toughness, notches, shafts, keys, belts, chains, couplings, journal bearings, spur gears and Lewis bending stress), pumps and water turbines, induction motors, three-phase power, star–delta conversion, transformers and power factor capacitors, Wheatstone bridges, thermocouples, radiant heat loss, cutting speeds and Taylor tool life.
- **Data Logger and Instrument Panel:** a channel's name, unit, scale or offset, and a panel widget's range, limit, decimals and step, are no longer put back by the next reading while you type them; what you type is kept until Enter or until you leave the box.
- **Command line:** one `-s` takes several settings (`-s crf=28 height=720`), as the help and the documentation have always written them; before, only the first was read and the rest were taken for file names.

- **Online, only when you ask:** PDF Toolbox's timestamp is the one new feature that uses the network. In the desktop app, with "Allow online checks" on, it sends a fingerprint of the PDF, never the PDF itself, to the RFC 3161 server you choose.
- Licence notices cover what these tools bring: D3 (ISC); anime.js, StPageFlip, Rough.js, perfect-freehand, rp2040js, nerdamer-prime, Sucrase and wasmoon (MIT); Pyodide (MPL-2.0) with CPython (PSF-2.0); Lua (MIT); libheif-js with libheif and libde265 (LGPL-3.0); the Kalam font (OFL-1.1); the RP2040 boot ROM (BSD-3-Clause, with GPL-2.0 parts); and the Adafruit GFX font (BSD-2-Clause).

## 5.0.0 (2026-11-24)

13 new tools, bringing the total to 403, with larger versions of four others: projects and searching across your files, visual workflows, studios for PDFs, print and vector drawing, a live quiz for a room full of phones, a green screen editor, and an electronics bench with a logic analyser and instrument panels.

- **Animation Studio:** SVG layers, motion paths, groups, clips with timelines of their own and a soundtrack in step; saves MP4, WebM and GIF video (through FFmpeg in the desktop app) besides a web page and Theatre.js state.
- **Slide Deck:** slides can be designed by hand, dragging text, pictures and shapes into place, as well as written in Markdown.
- **Green Screen Editor (new):** removes a green or blue backdrop from a picture or a video and puts your own picture, video or colour behind the subject, with tolerance, soft edge, tint removal and a live matte. Saves PNG, and MP4 or WebM through FFmpeg in the desktop app.
- **SVG.js Editor (new):** SVG.js code beside the drawing it makes, run in a sealed frame without network access; saves SVG, PNG or a web page that runs on its own.
- **Live Quiz (new):** a quiz or poll for a room: people join on their phones over your own Wi-Fi (desktop app) and answer while the big screen shows the results. Sixteen kinds of slide, among them scored multiple choice, typed answers, matching, ordering and sorting into categories, polls, word clouds, ratings, ranking, pins on a picture, Q&A, brainstorms, a spinner wheel and a leaderboard; teams, a countdown, secret ballots, results as CSV. - **Logic Analyser (new):** digital captures as waveforms with cursors, frequency and duty, and decoders for I²C, SPI and serial (UART) traffic with a table to save as CSV. Opens VCD files, sigrok sessions and tables; captures from logic analysers that speak SUMP over a serial port. A sketch included with it, the Logic Probe, makes an Arduino Uno or Nano into a six-line analyser (to 2 MHz, 1536 samples); it can be tried on an emulated Uno without a board.
- **Instrument Panel (new):** dials, bars, read-outs, lamps and trends for the values a board prints on its serial port, with buttons, switches and sliders that send it commands. Panels are saved as files; a pretend device and an example Arduino sketch are included.
- **Circuit Simulator:** the sketch on a simulated Arduino Uno or ESP32 can be compiled for a real board and uploaded to it with arduino-cli (desktop app), and the simulated Uno's pins can be recorded and opened in the Logic Analyser, serial text included.

## 4.1.1 (2026-11-23)

- **AI tools set up again:** the ONNX Runtime archives on Microsoft's GitHub releases page can no longer be downloaded (they answer HTTP 404), so Benchtop 4.0 and 4.1.0 could not install the runtime the AI tools need. ONNX Runtime 1.22.0 now comes from Microsoft's official packages on PyPI, which carry the same library, with this project's mirror as the fallback; every file is still checked against a fixed SHA-256 before use. A runtime installed by an earlier version stays installed and is not downloaded again.
- A failed download names PyPI when that is the source that failed.
- `scripts/mirror-models.py` reports a file it cannot fetch and carries on with the rest, and now copies the uv downloads too.
