Benchtop Coated FOGRA39L (public/icc/Benchtop-Coated-FOGRA39L.icc)

This ICC profile is made by Benchtop itself (scripts/make-cmyk-profile.mjs)
from the FOGRA39L characterization data: 1617 measured patches of offset
printing on coated paper (ISO 12647-2:2004/Amd 1, paper type 1 or 2),
originated by Fogra (www.fogra.org) in December 2006.

The data are taken from the colord project
(https://github.com/hughsie/colord, data/profiles/FOGRA39L_coated.iccprofile.xml),
where they are published with the statements "This profile is free of known
copyright restrictions" and "license: CC0". They are kept in this project as
scripts/data/FOGRA39L.ti3.

The profile is released the same way: no copyright, use freely
(CC0 1.0 Universal, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
The full text of CC0 1.0 is in icc/LICENSE.txt beside the profile.

"Fogra" and "FOGRA39" are names of Fogra Forschungsinstitut für Medientechnologien e.V.
This profile is not made, checked or endorsed by Fogra; it is a general-purpose
profile for the FOGRA39L printing condition, not a replacement for the profile
a printer names for a contract job.
